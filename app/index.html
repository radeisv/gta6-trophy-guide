55 trophies

    The road to the platinum takes about 200 hours at difficulty 4/10, across a story of 5 chapters that runs 60–80 hours. One playthrough is enough: after the credits the game stays open in free-roam, and there is no mission replay — so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so leave them off for a trophy run. The 100% checklist opens after the story at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion; it is closer to 60% true completion, and only 70 of the 80 challenges are needed. Work the four phases in order — the table below lists all 55 trophies with their missable flag, key requirement and source.
